## Respect and professionalism

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with
respect at all times. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any
kind are not tolerated and will be investigated promptly and confidentially
if reported.

## Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential company information, including
customer data, financial information, and unreleased product plans.
Confidential information should not be shared outside the company, or
internally beyond what is needed to do one's job, without proper
authorization.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees should disclose any outside activities, financial interests, or
relationships that could reasonably be seen as a conflict of interest with
their role at the company, such as working for a competitor or having a
financial stake in a vendor.

## Reporting concerns

Employees who witness or experience a violation of this code of conduct
should report it to their manager or HR. Reports can also be made
anonymously through the company's ethics hotline. Retaliation against
anyone who reports a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited.
