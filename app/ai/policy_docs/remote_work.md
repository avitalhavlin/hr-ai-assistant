## Standard remote work allowance

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week without prior approval,
as long as their team's core collaboration hours are covered. Remote days
should be coordinated with the employee's direct manager and team calendar
to avoid every team member being remote on the same day.

## Fully remote arrangements

Employees who wish to work remotely more than 2 days per week, or fully
remotely, must submit a request to their manager and HR. Fully remote
arrangements are evaluated on a role-by-role basis and are not guaranteed;
roles that require regular in-person collaboration or on-site presence may
not be eligible.

## Equipment and expenses

The company provides a laptop and standard peripherals for remote work.
Employees working remotely more than 3 days per week are eligible for a
one-time home office stipend, subject to manager approval, to cover items
such as a desk, chair, or monitor.

## Working hours while remote

Employees working remotely are still expected to be reachable and follow
the company's standard office hours and working days, unless a different
schedule has been explicitly agreed with their manager.
