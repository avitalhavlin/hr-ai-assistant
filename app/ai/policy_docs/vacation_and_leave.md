## Vacation days

Full-time employees accrue 21 vacation days per calendar year, credited at
the start of the year. Vacation days do not carry over to the next year —
any unused days are forfeited on December 31st. Employees can check their
current remaining balance through the HR assistant at any time.

## Sick leave

Employees are entitled to up to 10 paid sick days per calendar year.
Sick leave does not need to be requested in advance; employees should
notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible on the day of absence.
A doctor's note is required for sick leave lasting more than 3 consecutive
days.

## Parental leave

Employees who have completed at least 6 months of continuous employment are
eligible for 16 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth or adoption
of a child. Parental leave can be split into up to two separate periods
within the first year after the birth or adoption date.

## Requesting time off

Vacation requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for
approval by a manager. Requests submitted with less notice may still be
approved at the manager's discretion, particularly for emergencies.
